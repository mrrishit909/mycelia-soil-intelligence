# Color system: soil, root and sun

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Soil Black | #18140F | Page, canvas clear colour, fog, the bottom of the health ramp |
| Mycelium Cream | #EFE5CB | Text, outlines, the fungal strands' resting colour, cereal roots |
| Moss | #536B3C | Rail trunk, selected season, mid health |
| Fern | #92A85E | Legume roots, scenario line, good health, buttons |
| Clay | #A35C3B | Poor health, current-practice line, autumn ground |
| Sunlight | #E8C86A | Activation glow, the nutrient particles, best health, the active station |

The health ramp runs soil black (0) to clay (30) to moss (55) to fern (75) to sunlight (100), so the map reads as a soil moisture-to-light gradient rather than a traffic light. Season changes the sun's colour and elevation, the ground colour and the crop height.
