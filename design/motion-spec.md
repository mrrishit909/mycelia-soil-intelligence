# Motion spec

- Charts grow like roots: each carbon line draws with an animated stroke from the base, with short twigs sprouting at every other year. Reduced motion shows them fully grown.
- Filter state spreads like mycelium: matching fields light at once; the glow reaches their neighbours one hop at a time (0.35 s per hop) and the links between fields brighten as both ends light.
- Field to field navigation travels underground: a pulse runs along the strands, then the view moves to the neighbour.
- Water is restrained: 400 rain points above the block while a storm plays, and moisture bands that fade in layer by layer from the simulated hourly moisture.
- Season changes the sun elevation and colour, the ground colour, and the height of the crop cones.
- Panels grow in from their top edge (clip-path), not slide.
